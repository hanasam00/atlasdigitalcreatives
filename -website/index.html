(04) Frequently asked questions

Before we get
started.

A few helpful details about the design process, revisions, communication, and final files.

01 What do you need to get started?

I’ll need your business name, a short description of your brand, preferred colors, required text, and any references or inspiration you would like me to consider.

02 Can you match my brand style or references?

Yes. I can design around your existing branding and visual references while keeping the final result original and tailored specifically to your business.

03 Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Revisions are included based on your selected service or package. Additional revisions can also be purchased if more changes are needed.

04 How long will my project take?

Turnaround time depends on the project’s size, complexity, and my current availability. You’ll receive an estimated timeline before work begins.

05 What file formats will I receive?

Final files are supplied in the formats appropriate for your project. These may include PNG, JPG, PDF, or SVG files for digital, social media, and print use.

06 What is a vector file and why is it important?

A vector file, such as an SVG, can be resized without becoming blurry or losing quality. This makes it ideal for logos, websites, signs, merchandise, and printed materials.

07 How does payment work?

Pricing and payment details are confirmed before the project begins. A deposit may be required to reserve your project and begin the design process.

08 Do you accept rush projects?

Rush projects may be accepted depending on availability and may include an additional fee. Contact me with your deadline so I can confirm whether it can be accommodated.

09 What is the best way to contact you?

The best way to reach me is by email at atlasdigitalcreatives0@gmail.com or through Facebook Messenger. Please include a short description of what you need and your preferred timeline.
